Why it matters

Rewire your brain

Writing down positive moments trains your mind to notice more of them. Gratitude journalling is one of the most evidence-backed wellbeing habits.

Your brain is wired to remember bad things more vividly. Happy tilts the scales back — a record of good that your future self can actually read.
